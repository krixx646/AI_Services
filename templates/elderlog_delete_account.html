Delete your ElderLog AI account

Google Play requires a way to request account deletion without installing the app. Use either path below.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open ElderLog AI and sign in
  2. Go to Settings
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm

Deletion removes your account profile and personal care data from our servers and this device.

  • If you are the only member of a care circle, that circle is deleted.
  • If other members remain, shared care logs stay for them; your membership is removed.
  • This cannot be undone.

Without the app

Email krixxvalle@gmail.com from the address on your account with subject:

ElderLog account deletion

Include the email you used to sign in. We aim to complete deletion within 30 days.

Subscriptions

Premium is billed by Google Play. Cancel or manage billing in Play → Payments & subscriptions. Deleting your ElderLog account does not automatically cancel a Play subscription.
