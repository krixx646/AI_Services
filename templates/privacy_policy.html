Privacy Policy for Xpenze: AI Budget Tracker

Effective date: March 6, 2026

Xpenze ("we", "our", "us") provides an expense and budget tracking app with optional cloud sync, ads for free users, and premium subscription features.

1) Information We Collect

a) Account data: email address, display name, profile photo (if provided).

b) Financial records you manually enter: manually entered figures, categories, budgets, transactions, recurring rules, tags, and notes.

Xpenze is not a fintech app. It does not connect to bank/fintech accounts, request banking login credentials, or automatically import transactions from financial institutions.

c) Optional attachments: receipt reference/metadata when you attach receipts.

d) Notification capture data (optional): supported transaction notification details such as merchant name, amount, time, and related text, which may be turned into draft transactions for your review.

e) App and device data: app version, basic diagnostics, analytics events, and identifiers needed for app security and fraud prevention.

f) Subscription/payment data: purchase tokens, subscription status, and entitlement state from Google Play Billing (we do not store your full card details).

2) Notification Capture And Drafts

If you enable notification capture, Xpenze may use Android notification access to read supported transaction notifications on your device and extract details such as merchant name, amount, time, and related text into a draft transaction for your review. Notification capture is optional and can be turned off at any time in the app or in Android settings. Xpenze does not move money, approve payments, or automatically post captured notifications as transactions. Captured drafts remain separate from your transaction history until you review and save them.

3) Cloud Sync

If you sign in and enable cloud-backed use of the app, Xpenze may sync your accounts, categories, budgets, recurring rules, and saved transactions with your cloud account so they can be restored across devices.

4) Mochi

If you use Mochi, Xpenze may send your prompt and relevant financial context, such as transaction, budget, recurring, and account summary data, to backend AI services in order to generate a response.

5) Ads And Analytics

Xpenze uses analytics and advertising SDKs, including Google services, to understand app usage, improve features, and show ads on the free plan.

6) How We Use Information

  • Provide core app features (tracking, budgets, reports, reminders).
  • Create draft transactions from supported notifications when notification capture is enabled.
  • Sync your data across devices when cloud-backed use is enabled.
  • Generate Mochi responses using your prompt and relevant financial context.
  • Enable and verify premium subscriptions and trial eligibility.
  • Improve app quality, reliability, and security.
  • Show ads to free-plan users.

7) Services We Use

We may process data through trusted service providers, including:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Functions, Analytics, App Check)
  • Google Play Billing
  • Google AdMob
  • Backend AI services used by Mochi and related finance coaching features

8) Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We only share data with service providers required to operate the app, legal authorities when required by law, or to protect users and platform security.

9) Data Retention

We keep data while your account is active or as needed to provide the service. You can request deletion at any time (see Section 11).

10) Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards. No system is 100% secure, but we continuously improve protections.

11) Your Rights and Data Deletion

You can delete your account from inside the app:

Settings -> Danger Zone -> Delete Account

If you cannot access the app, request deletion via:

Email: krixxvalle@gmail.com

Subject: "Xpenze Data Deletion Request"

Please include your account email and user ID (if available). We process verified requests within a reasonable period (typically within 30 days).

12) Children

Xpenze is not directed to children under 13 (or equivalent minimum age in your region).

13) Changes to this Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date.

14) Contact

For privacy questions: Email: krixxvalle@gmail.com
