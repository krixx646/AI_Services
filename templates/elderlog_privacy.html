ElderLog AI — Privacy Policy

Effective / last updated: 2026-09-09

App: ElderLog AI Caregiver Log (com.chelumore.elderlogai) on Google Play

Developer contact: krixxvalle@gmail.com

1. Who we are and what this app is

ElderLog AI is a care coordination and logging app for adult family caregivers. You can speak or type care updates (medication given, meals, vitals readings you record, incidents, notes), confirm them, share them with an invited care circle, and generate a doctor/hospital coordination packet.

ElderLog AI is not a medical device. It does not diagnose conditions, prescribe or dose medication, provide clinical advice, or replace a licensed clinician or emergency services. If someone needs urgent medical help, call local emergency services.

2. Data we collect

Category Examples Purpose
Account Email, display name, authentication identifiers Sign-in (Firebase Auth), caregiver identity
Care circle Group name, invite code, member roles, elder/care-recipient name Family coordination
Care events Content you confirm (voice transcript drafts, structured fields, timestamps); optional photo label text / image paths for Premium photo+voice Sync, handoff briefs, PDF packets, Copilot (Premium)
Device / prefs App language, notification preferences, local premium entitlement cache App behavior
Push tokens FCM device token Care-update and handoff notifications (when enabled)
Billing Google Play subscription / purchase status Premium entitlements (processed by Google)
Ads (Free tier) AdMob advertising / request identifiers Non-invasive banner ads

AI processing: Voice transcripts (and, for Premium Copilot, chat messages / tool context) may be sent to our Supabase Edge Functions, which call Google Gemini to draft structured logs or coordination replies. Care logs are not saved until you confirm in the app. We do not use your care logs to train public AI models.

3. How we store and who processes data

  • On your device: local Room database cache and preference stores
  • Supabase: care groups, members, patients, care events, optional care-photo storage (with access controls / RLS)
  • Firebase (Google): Authentication, Cloud Messaging
  • Google Play Billing: subscriptions
  • Google AdMob: Free-tier ads
  • Google Gemini (via our Edge Functions): drafting assistance only

Data may be processed on servers in regions used by these providers (including the United States and other locations where those services operate).

4. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only:

  • With members of your care circle (the care events and circle details you participate in)
  • With processors listed above, as needed to run the app
  • When required by law or to protect safety / rights

5. Retention

  • Account and care-circle data are kept while your account / circle membership is active.
  • Local caches remain on device until you clear app data or uninstall.
  • After account deletion requests are completed, we delete or anonymize personal data we control, except where we must retain limited records for legal, security, or abuse-prevention reasons.

6. Your choices and rights

In the app you can:

  • Export care data: Settings → Export my data
  • Leave a care circle / sign out
  • Change language and notification preferences
  • Manage system notification permission in Android settings
  • Upgrade / cancel Premium via Google Play subscription management

Account deletion: See the account deletion page, or email krixxvalle@gmail.com from the address on your account with subject “ElderLog account deletion”. We aim to complete deletion within 30 days.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or export personal data, or to object to certain processing. Contact us to exercise those rights.

7. Children

ElderLog AI is intended for adult caregivers (13+; typically 18+) coordinating care for elders. It is not directed at children under 13 as primary users. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

We use industry-standard providers, encrypted transport (HTTPS), and access controls (including database row-level security for circle data). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

9. Permissions (Android)

Depending on features you use, the app may request:

  • Microphone — voice care logs
  • Camera — Premium photo + voice label assist
  • Notifications — care updates / handoff digest
  • Internet — sync, auth, ads, AI drafting
